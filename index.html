Getting started

How we bring you on board.

Onboarding follows three steps before any filing work begins, so nothing is set up on an assumption.

1

Identify requirements

We start by understanding your business: its structure, transactions, and which registrations and filings actually apply to you.

2

Collect your data

We gather the financial records, documents, and access needed to set up your books and filings correctly from day one.

3

Register & follow up

We complete any pending registrations on your behalf and follow up until each one is confirmed, then move you into regular filing.

Compliance calendar

The dates we watch for you.

Standard monthly and quarterly deadlines for a typical GST-registered business. We track these so you don't have to.

7th

TDS deposit

Tax deducted in the previous month deposited with the government.

11th

GSTR-1

Monthly statement of outward supplies for the previous month.

20th

GSTR-3B

Monthly summary return and payment of GST liability.

Qtrend + 1

TDS returns

Quarterly TDS statements (24Q / 26Q) filed after each quarter closes.

Due dates shown are the standard ones and may change with government notifications or your filing category. We confirm the exact dates that apply to you during onboarding.
